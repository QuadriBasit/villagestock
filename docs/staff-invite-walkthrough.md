# Staff invite walkthrough (one person, one browser)

Use this when sitting with a client. Do not test the invite in the same Chrome profile as the shop owner.

## Owner (shop already set up)

1. Settings → Team → People.
2. Type the teammate’s **exact** email (the Google they will use).
3. Send invitation. Confirm the toast and that a **new** mail arrived.
4. If you send again, only the **latest** mail works. Old inbox links die.

## Teammate (different phone or Incognito)

1. Open the **newest** VillageStock invite mail only.
2. Open the join link (`/join?invite=…`).
3. Sign in with **that same email** (Google or first-time password).
4. Success: they land on **this shop’s dashboard**, not “Tell us about your shop.”

## Do not

- Open the invite while still signed in as the owner.
- Use a different Gmail than the one you typed.
- Reuse an older invite email after you clicked Send again.

If the teammate already has their own VillageStock shop, they still join **this** shop after accepting. Use a different browser profile so leftover invite links do not sit on the owner’s phone.

## If it says the emails do not match

Sign out and sign in with the invited address. The link never attaches a random Google account to the shop.
